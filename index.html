How this is worked out

Plain arithmetic on the two buys.

  • The buy price is each ETF's NZX closing price on the day of investing.
  • Units = amount invested ÷ buy price. Value = units × latest price.
  • Brokerage fees, distributions and tax aren't included.
  • Prices come from NZX (nzx.com), about twenty minutes delayed.
